# PETRICHOR-weather-app

*weather with a personality*

Petrichor shows the forecast for any city around the globe, wrapped in a soft pink interface with a bit of personality. It doesn't just report the numbers — it tells you what to wear, what to do, and how the day is going to feel, from right now through the week ahead.

## Overview

Petrichor is a weather app built around one idea: weather data is more useful when it feels human. Alongside the standard temperature and conditions, every city gets a short, mood-driven description of the day, plus practical suggestions for what to wear and what to do. The whole interface is wrapped in a soft pink theme that sets it apart from typical weather apps.

## Features

### Search & Location
- 🌍 **Global city search** — look up current conditions and forecasts for any city, anywhere (Tokyo, Nairobi, Buenos Aires, and everywhere in between)
- 📍 **Use my location** — instantly pull up the weather for wherever you are

### Current Conditions
- 🌡️ **Celsius / Fahrenheit toggle** — switch between units with a single tap
- 📊 **At-a-glance stats** — humidity, wind speed & direction, high/low, and UV index
- 🗣️ **Personality-driven descriptions** — every day gets its own one-line mood, like *"Crisp, starry, cinematic."*
- ⭐ **Favorite cities** — star a city to save it for quick access later

### Suggestions
- 👕 **What to wear** — clothing suggestions based on today's weather
- 🚶 **What to do** — activity suggestions suited to the day's conditions
- ✨ **Honest takes** — the app doesn't just list stats, it tells you plainly whether it's worth going outside

### Forecast
- 🌧️ **Rain outlook** — a 12-hour rain probability chart with a clearly marked peak chance
- 🕐 **Hourly forecast** — a scrollable 24-hour outlook with temperature and rain chance for each hour
- 📅 **7-day forecast** — a full week ahead with condition icons, daily rain probability, and a visual low/high temperature range bar for each day

### Design
- 🎀 **Pink-themed interface** — a soft, cohesive visual identity carried through every screen
- 🧭 **Clean, card-based layout** — current conditions, suggestions, and forecasts are organized into distinct, scannable sections
